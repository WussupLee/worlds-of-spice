Recording: 1977 Gottlieb Bronco machine; two Neumann KM 184 microphones,
XY stereo, Scarlett 18i20 preamp. These are mechanical field recordings,
not extracted commercial game ROM audio or synthesized substitutes.

Retrieved September 24, 2026 from the publicly served HQ MP3 previews linked
in each source page. Original 96 kHz WAV downloads require an account; these
assets are edited preview derivatives, not claimed to be the original WAVs.
Public preview pattern: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/450/{id}_98337-hq.mp3

Edits: selected short isolated events; mono 44.1 kHz PCM; 90 Hz high-pass,
6.5 kHz low-pass, short edge fades, peak normalized to 0.72. Source times and
the reproducible processing pipeline are in scripts/prepare-mechanics.mjs.
Runtime changes: quiet gain, small pitch variation, distance filtering,
stereo positioning and a shared original 3.4-second convolution reverb.
